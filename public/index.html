THE POWER OF CONNECTION

A place to
belong.

At Eshbal, young people find a place to belong. In our kennel and stable, they also discover what it means to be needed. Feeding, listening, showing up — small acts of care become a daily practice of trust and responsibility.

Our dogs and horses are part of that story. The stable and kennel are already active. Now we’re raising $500,000 for a protected room, practical learning and a sustainable future for the whole center.

WHAT WE’RE BUILDING

One room.
Two purposes.

Our stable and kennel are already built and operating. The next essential step is a protected room for the young people and staff of both programs. In daily life, that same space will become a professional dog grooming salon and a place to learn practical skills.

THE GROOMING SALON

Everyday care.
Skills for a lifetime.

A professional setting to learn bathing, brushing, coat care and safe handling. Practical experience can become the first step toward vocational training and future employment.

  • Hands-on learning with dogs
  • Space for professional grooming equipment
  • A foundation for future work
THE PROTECTED SPACE

A safe place.
A steadier tomorrow.

During an emergency, the same room will provide a protected space for young people and staff from the kennel and stable. Safety helps preserve the relationships and routines they rely on.

  • Protection for participants and staff
  • Serving both the stable and kennel
  • Supporting educational continuity

ABOUT ESHBAL

A community.
A home.

Eshbal is a kibbutz of educators in the Galilee, part of Dror Israel. For young people who live here, it offers a caring residential community and the opportunity for a fresh start.

The center grows out of that everyday life. Dedicated educators, shared responsibility and meaningful relationships surround the work with dogs and horses. Learning continues beyond a session in the stable or kennel.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Your questions,
answered.

Can I spread my commitment over three years?+

Yes. The campaign’s organizational framework through Dror Israel (Dror Educational Houses) allows commitments to be paid over as many as three years. Contact Gilad to agree on a schedule and the appropriate payment route.

How can I give from the United States or Canada?+

You can use the online donation form. For questions about giving routes or receipt arrangements in your country, contact Gilad.

Are there opportunities for a leadership gift?+

Yes. We welcome conversations about founding partnerships and major gifts. A limited number of major naming opportunities may be considered for gifts of $100,000 or more. Recognition will be thoughtful and restrained, in keeping with the spirit of Eshbal.

Who manages the campaign’s donations?+

Project donations are managed through Dror Educational Houses — Hechalutz. Gilad can provide the relevant giving information and help ensure your gift is designated for the Eshbal center. View organization documents ↗
